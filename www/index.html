    Listening & conversation

      Conversational vocabulary

      While a prompt is pending (e.g. "Say confirm to arm"), only these responses are accepted: confirm, execute, yes, affirmative, proceed, go ahead — accept; cancel, no, abort, negative, never mind — reject; hold, pause, stop, halt, wait — reject the pending action and issue that action instead. Anything else during a pending prompt is ignored so an unrelated sentence can't accidentally confirm a motor command.
